Tuwaiq Academy

Tuwaiq Academy is an educational institution based in Riyadh. The Saudi Federation for Cybersecurity, Programming, and Drones announced its establishment in August 2019 and is headquartered at Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University. It is the first advanced technology academy in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, offering high-level advanced technology training programs and boot camps in programming, software engineering, data science, and cybersecurity.

Doroob

It is a comprehensive program sponsored by the Human Resources Development Fund (HRDF). It begins by offering online training, then proceeds to provide accredited certificates recognized by major companies in the Kingdom, which give priority in direct employment to those holding these certificates.
